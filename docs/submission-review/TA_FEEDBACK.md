# Assignment 2 proposal feedback

The project owner supplied the following feedback in the October 21, 2026 proposal-revision conversation. The wording below follows that transcription. The [team response](Response_to_Proposal_Feedback.docx) records the changes proposed at the time.

> -1pt for lack of specific goals
>
> Ultimately the proposal hits a lot of the requirements for the Assignment, however I would recommend you re-consider your project focus for the reasons discussed below.
>
> - The basic comparison of Normative vs Constructive frameworks is insufficient for a course project and is overly redundant with Assignment 1. We have already compared and contrasted the normative vs constructive theories in class and in a prior essay.
>
> - The quantitative comparison of normative vs constructive approaches for a specific case study is a more valid project. However, you currently only have 50% of the effort dedicated to case study research and analysis (timeline items 3 & 4) but I think these should be a much bigger focus, if not _the_ focus of the project.
>
> - Additionally, constructive approaches are designed around the idea that you have iterative stakeholder feedback. This is challenging if not impossible when you don't have a realistic stakeholder.
>
> I would recommend:
> - Spend significantly time thinking about your case study along with clear justification for _why_ this is chosen. This motivation & context will be important and is currently lacking in your project. Try identifying theoretical projects or real-world current projects (not historical projects).
>
> - Get more specific with the methods being considered. I would recommend looking more at the syllabus to consider multiple different approaches to compare and contrast. Currently, the proposal is unclear with respect to what methods are going to be used.
>
> Come by Office Hours if you need to get more feedback or guidance.
